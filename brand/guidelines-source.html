Tap Am brand guidelines02
01 · Who we are

Tap fast. Win am.

Tap Am is a live tapping game for Nigeria. Players join pools, tap as fast as they can against the clock and other players, climb 100 ranks and win prizes. It is loud, quick and proudly Naija.

The brand should feel like a game show on your phone: bold colour, chunky shapes, big italic type, a cheeky Pidgin voice — and total clarity whenever money is involved.

Personality

Playful
Banter, emojis in moderation, Pidgin jokes. Never mean.
Fast
Short words, big buttons, one idea per screen.
Proudly Naija
Lagos slang, local food, local names, ₦ everywhere.
Straight about money
Clear fees, clear rules, no promises of income.
Tagline
Tap ammm make you
chop moneyyyy

The tagline is written the way it is said — stretched letters and all. On the landing page it appears as “tap ammm make you chop moneyyyy”; the share image uses “Tap amm make you chop big moneyyy”. Pick one spelling and use it everywhere (we recommend the landing-page one).

Important: the tagline is a joke, not a promise. Our Terms say slogans are not promises of income or prizes. Never use it next to claims like “guaranteed”, “easy money” or “earn daily”.

02 · The logo

Our wordmark

“TAP” in heavy italic, “AM” knocked out of a rounded box. The slant is motion — a finger mid-tap.

  • Primary (light backgrounds): black TAP, Logo Purple #AA0FEA box, white AM.
  • Reverse (dark backgrounds): white TAP, purple box, white AM. This is the version in the app header.
  • The logo is drawn as shapes, not typed. Always use the files in the kit — never retype it in a font.
  • Say it “Tap Am”. Write it Tap Am in sentences (capital T, capital A, one space).

Files: 01-logo/svg/logo-primary-purple.svg, logo-reverse-white-purple.svg (PNG at 600, 1200 and 2400 px wide in 01-logo/png).

05 · The finger mark & app icon

One finger, ready to tap

The finger is our shortcut — app icon, favicon, stickers, loading spots and anywhere the full logo won’t fit.

  • App icon: white finger on Mark Green #006012, rounded square. Never redraw it.
  • On its own, the finger can be Mark Green, Tap Green, Ink or white.
  • Don’t put the finger next to the full logo — they’re alternatives, not partners.

Use Black Italic (900) or ExtraBold Italic (800) for headlines and big numbers; Black (900) upright for buttons; Bold (700) for small labels. Set tight: line height 0.9–1.05.

Meet the tappers

Twenty rank characters — one for each tier, from Fresh Finger to Tap Am Legend. Use them in social posts, rank-up moments and merch. Always show them in a circle, never cropped or recoloured.

Badges: a star means most taps, ₦ means most prize money won. Stickers and badges use a thick Ink outline and flat fills.

12 · Voice & tone

Talk like a Lagos friend

Light Pidgin, short sentences, a wink. We sound like the friend who hypes you up — and who tells you the rules straight.

MomentSay
Call to play“Tap fast, chop prize.” · “Run am back!”
Win“Odogwu! You win am!” · “₦1,000 don land for your wallet!”
Loss“No prize for you this time — run am back!”
Error“That page no load. Try again.” (say what to do next)
Money rulesPlain English: “Money you add can’t be withdrawn. Only winnings can.”
Do
  • Use ₦ with commas: ₦10,000 (not N10000 or 10k naira).
  • Name the tiers: Lapo baby, Mapo baby, Nepo baby.
  • Switch to plain English for fees, refunds, age (18+) and legal notes.
  • Celebrate players — rank-ups, badges, top winners.
Don’t
  • Promise income: no “guaranteed”, “easy money”, “earn daily”, “get rich”.
  • Target or show anyone under 18 in money promotions.
  • Mock other tribes, religions, players or competitors.
  • Overdo it: one Pidgin line per message is plenty.
Money stakes and paid prizes are still under legal review. Marketing that talks about cash prizes should be checked by your lawyer before it goes out.
